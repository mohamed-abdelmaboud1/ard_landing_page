The Authentic Marketplace

What Makes The Ard Different?

The Ard is not just another travel app. It is Egypt’s first nationwide authentic marketplace that connects conscious travelers directly with real Egyptian hosts, artisans, and experience providers.

01

Preferred Host Stories

Every partner gets to tell their own real story (photos + video). These stories become the heart of the app and our marketing.

02

Golden Hands

A dedicated space to showcase Egypt’s masterpieces — handmade crafts, traditional products, and local creativity.

03

Nationwide Coverage

We are building the platform for all of Egypt, not just tourist hotspots.

04

Fair & Transparent Model

  • 10% commission on services (you keep 90%)
  • 20% commission on Golden Hands products (you keep 80%)
  • Weekly payments via Paymob (fast & reliable)
